The Problem

🧩 Knowledge Gaps

Students move on to advanced topics without mastering the basics, causing compounding confusion.

📚 One-Size-Fits-All

Traditional teaching can't adapt to every learner's pace, language, or preferred format.

🔍 Research Chaos

Notes are scattered across apps. Students can't quickly find, query, or synthesize their own material.

🗣️ Language Barriers

Quality educational content is predominantly in English, leaving millions of regional-language learners behind.

Personalized Learning

Why-Path starts with a short diagnostic quiz to find exactly where your knowledge gaps and missing prerequisites are — then builds a step-by-step learning path just for you.

🎯 Diagnostic Quiz

A quick, concept-level assessment pinpoints strengths and weaknesses before you even start studying.

🤖 AI Mentor

An always-available mentor continuously tracks your strong and weak topics and adjusts your path.

🌐 Regional Languages

Audio, subtitles, quizzes, and short reel-style lessons — all in your preferred regional language.

👩‍🏫 Teacher Insights

Teachers get dashboards showing commonly missed concepts across their classes.

Smart Research Assistant — "Notebook"

Upload or paste your notes and chat with an AI that answers from your own material, with numbered source references.

💬 Chat with Your Notes

Ask questions and get answers grounded in your uploaded material — never hallucinated facts.

📑 Source References

Every answer includes numbered citations so you can verify and trace back to the original text.

📝 Smart Outputs

Auto-generated summaries, study guides, FAQs, smart notes, and suggested follow-up questions.

The 7-Step Learning Cycle

A structured loop that turns passive studying into active, measurable learning.

  1. 1

    Take the Quiz

    A short diagnostic assessment on your chosen topic.

  2. 2

    Find Gaps

    AI identifies the specific concepts and prerequisites you're missing.

  3. 3

    Get a Learning Path

    A personalized, step-by-step study plan is created for you.

  4. 4

    Upload Materials

    Add your own notes, textbooks, or lecture slides to the Notebook.

  5. 5

    Ask the AI

    Chat with your material — get answers with numbered source references.

  6. 6

    Summaries & Notes

    Generate study guides, FAQs, smart notes, and suggested questions.

  7. 7

    Practise & Track

    Practise with targeted exercises and track your progress over time.

    What Makes Why-Path Unique?

    🔬 Concept-Level Precision

    We don't just grade answers right or wrong — we diagnose which concept is weak and which prerequisite is missing.

    📖 Your Material, Your Answers

    The Notebook answers only from your uploaded content, with verifiable numbered references — no hallucinations.

    🌍 Truly Multilingual

    Audio, subtitles, quizzes, and reel-style lessons in regional languages — not just translated text.

    ♻️ Closed-Loop Learning

    The 7-step cycle ensures continuous improvement: diagnose → learn → practise → re-diagnose.

    👩‍🏫 Teacher & Institution Ready

    Dashboards surface commonly missed concepts, helping teachers target instruction where it matters most.

    ⚡ No Lock-In

    Works with any curriculum and any subject. Students bring their own notes; the AI adapts to them.
